# Boards

A board is a workspace: one project, one team, one list of things to do. It holds
areas, and the areas hold cards. Everything else in LokalBoards hangs off a
board.

![The dashboard, showing four boards with their menus](/images/docs/dashboard.webp)

The dashboard lists every board you own or have been invited to. Boards you were
invited to carry a **Shared** badge, and the avatars on a tile are the people who
can see it.

Every tile carries a **⋮** of its own, holding the same things the board's own
page offers: its settings, who is on it, and deleting it — or, for a board
somebody shared with you, leaving it. Renaming a board or inviting somebody to it
does not need the board to be opened first.

![A board tile's menu, with settings, invite and delete](/images/docs/board-tile-menu.webp)

## Creating a board

Click the blue **+** at the top right. Give the board a name and save — you can
change everything else later.

A section with nothing in it — a dashboard with no boards yet, or a group you
have just made — shows a **+** tile of its own instead, and a board made from
that one lands in that section.

![The dialog for a new board, with its name, thumbnail, colour, style and privacy](/images/docs/board-create.webp)

The **⋮** button beside it holds **Import from Trello**, which recreates a public
Trello board here in full: lists, cards, descriptions, checklists, comments,
attachments and which cards were already finished.

Use **New group** at the bottom of the dashboard to sort boards into groups once
you have more than a screenful.

## Board options

Click the **⋮** on the board's tile, or open the board and click the **⋮** at the
top right. Either reaches the same options.

![The board options dialog: name, thumbnail, colour, style and status](/images/docs/board-options.webp)

**Thumbnail and colour** decide how the board's tile looks on the dashboard.
Pick one of the supplied images or upload your own, or pick one of the twelve
colours — or mix your own with the pipette. A tile shows one or the other:
choosing a colour clears the image, and choosing an image clears the colour.
Whichever you pick, the board's name, the avatars and the badges stay readable,
because the tile works out whether it needs light or dark text for the colour you
chose.

**Style** switches between **KanBan**, where areas stand side by side as columns,
and **ToDo**, where they stack full width as lists. It is the same board and the
same cards either way — see [Areas](/docs/areas) for when each one helps.

**Status** decides who can reach the board:

- **Private** — only you and the people you invite.
- **Public** — anyone with the link can open it.

Everyone looking at the board sees the change as it is saved, and so does every
dashboard that has a tile for it — a renamed board is renamed everywhere, and a
new colour arrives on the tile without anybody reloading.

## Sharing a board

Click **⋮ › Permissions** on a board to see who has access and to add someone.

![The permissions dialog, listing two members and the field for adding another](/images/docs/board-invite.webp)

Search for the person by name or e-mail address. If they already have an account
here, pick them from the list. If they do not, type their full e-mail address:
LokalBoards sends them an invitation to create an account, and signing up through
that link joins them to this board with the access you chose. The link works once
and expires after 14 days, and it carries the address it was sent to — nobody can
use it to register under a different one.

An invitation works even on an instance with public signup switched off, which is
the point: the board owner decides who joins, not the sign-up form.

Choose what they may do:

- **Read only** — they can open the board and follow it, but change nothing.
- **Read & write** — they can move cards, write comments and edit anything on it.

The list at the top shows everyone who already has access, with an eye or a
pencil for their permission. Use the pencil to change it and the bin to take it
away again.

## Everyone sees it at once

Boards are live. When somebody moves a card, renames an area, ticks something off
or writes a comment, everyone looking at that board sees it happen — no refresh,
no stale screen, and no two people quietly editing different versions of the same
thing.

If you are not at your desk when it happens, an e-mail goes out once an hour with
everything that changed on the boards you are on.

## Filtering what you see

The funnel beside the **⋮** narrows the board to the cards you want to look at:
by label, by who it is assigned to, by whether it is overdue or due today or due
this week or has no date at all, and by open or done. Picking two labels widens
the answer — cards wearing either one — while picking a label *and* an assignee
narrows it.

Each area's header counts its cards, and while a filter is on it counts both:
**3 / 12** means three of its twelve cards match. Nothing is moved or changed;
cards you filtered out are simply not drawn, and **Clear filters** brings them
back. The filter is yours and lasts as long as you are looking at the board.

## Archiving a board

**⋮ › Archive board**, then confirm. The board leaves your dashboard and
everybody else's, keeping its areas, cards, comments and attachments exactly as
they are.

Nothing is destroyed. **⋮ › Archive** on the dashboard lists what you have
archived and puts it back. An archived board still opens from a link or a
bookmark — it says at the top that it is archived, and its owner can restore it
from there.

The same applies inside a board: the button on an area's header archives the
area with the cards in it, and **Archive card** on an open card archives that
one. Both are listed in **⋮ › Archive** on the board, with the date they were
put away, and both come back exactly as they were.

Deleting something outright is still possible, and it lives in the archive: the
bin beside an entry removes it and everything belonging to it for good. That one
cannot be undone, which is why it is the only place in the app you will find it.
