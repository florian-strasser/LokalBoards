# Comments

Comments are the conversation about a card, kept on the card. The reason a due
date moved or a decision went the way it did belongs where the work is, not in a
chat channel where it scrolls away by Thursday.

![The comments and activity section of a card](/images/docs/card.webp)

## Writing one

Open a card, scroll to **Comments and activity**, click **Write a comment**, type
and post it. The comment appears immediately for everyone else looking at that
card — no refresh.

Your own comments can be edited or deleted afterwards with the pencil and bin
beside them.

## What else appears there

The section is called *Comments and activity* because it holds both. Comments
imported from another system keep the original author's name even though that
person has no account here, so a Trello board that comes across brings its
discussion with it rather than a wall of anonymous text.

## Who finds out

Writing a comment notifies the people on that board: the bell in the header
gets a dot, and the notification names the card so it is one click away. The
dashboard's [Activity](/docs/boards#what-happened-lately) page has all of them
together. If they are not at their desk, the hourly e-mail digest carries it
instead.

## Agents comment too

An assistant working the board through the API or the MCP server writes comments
like anyone else, and its account is marked with a bot icon. A card that was
updated by an agent never looks like a colleague did it.
